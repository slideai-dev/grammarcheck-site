Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Grammar Check app for iOS ("Grammar Check", "the app", "we") handles information. Grammar Check is published by an individual developer ("the developer"). By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

In short: Grammar Check does not require an account and does not sell your data. Text you check, rewrite or translate (in the app, with the Grammar Checker keyboard, in the Safari extension or in the iMessage app) is sent to our server and to Anthropic, and the result is returned to you. The app explains this in a notice called "How your text is checked"; the keyboard and the Safari extension send nothing until you agree to it. We do not keep your text. The app also includes third-party software that collects limited technical, usage and purchase information, described below.

1. Information we collect

Text you check in the app

  • Grammar checks, rewrites, translations and originality estimates. When you use these features, the text is sent over an encrypted connection to our server (hosted on Cloudflare) and from there to Anthropic, which generates the result. We do not store the text on our server after returning the result. The Originality Check is an AI estimate of how original the writing reads; it does not search the web or compare your text with other documents.
  • Your documents. Documents you write or import are stored on your device only. Text from a document is sent to our server only when you run a check or an originality estimate on it.
  • Scanning text from photos. When you scan text from a photo, the text is recognised on your device by Apple's Vision framework. The photo is not uploaded.

Grammar Checker keyboard

The keyboard is optional and works for plain typing without "Allow Full Access". It never sends anything unless you have turned on Full Access and agreed to "How your text is checked" in the app. With both:

  • About 1.5 seconds after you stop typing, the text in the field you are typing in is sent to our server and Anthropic so the keyboard can suggest fixes. Text you rewrite or translate with the keyboard's buttons is sent the same way.
  • In Reply mode, the keyboard reads your clipboard to see the message you are replying to. It does this only when you choose Reply.
  • Password fields and other secure fields never reach the keyboard; iOS switches to the system keyboard for them.
  • The keyboard stores your settings and the free daily rewrite count on your device.

Voice typing

When you tap the microphone on the keyboard, the Grammar Check app opens and records your speech with your permission. Speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition, which may process audio on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy. The transcript (not the audio) is then sent to our server and Anthropic to tidy up punctuation and filler words. We do not receive or keep your audio.

Safari extension

If you turn on the Grammar Checker extension in Safari and have agreed to "How your text is checked", the extension checks text you type into plain text fields and text areas on web pages after you pause, and sends that text to our server and Anthropic. It never reads password, email, search or URL fields. Until you agree in the app, it sends nothing.

iMessage app

When you use Grammar Check in Messages, the text you choose to check or rewrite is sent to our server and Anthropic, and the result is added to your message only when you tap to add it.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information, such as financial details or health information.

Information collected automatically by third-party software

Grammar Check includes third-party software development kits (SDKs). When you use the app, these SDKs may collect:

  • Device identifiers such as a randomly generated installation ID, the identifier for vendor (IDFV) and similar technical identifiers. Requests to our server carry the IDFV so we can apply fair-use limits, and requests for Pro features carry your anonymous RevenueCat ID so we can confirm your subscription. The app does not request or collect the advertiser ID (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Device and app information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, region, time zone and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • App usage events such as which screens are opened, which features are used, session length and interaction with the in-app paywall.
  • Purchase events such as when a subscription is started, renewed, cancelled or restored, the product purchased and the price. Payment details are handled by Apple and are never visible to us.

Information you send us

If you send feedback from the app or contact support by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. Feedback sent from the app includes the app version, device model, iOS version and app language, which you can see and edit before sending.

Information we do not collect

Grammar Check does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address (unless you contact us), precise location, contacts or health data. Text-to-speech playback and text scanning run on your device.

2. How we use information

  • To check, rewrite, translate and estimate the originality of the text you submit, and to deliver features you request.
  • To understand how the app is used so we can improve it.
  • To display the in-app paywall and confirm your subscription status.
  • To respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to the app.

3. Third-party services

The following third-party services receive the information described above. Each processes it under its own privacy policy.

  • Anthropic (Claude; generates corrections, rewrites, translations and originality estimates from the text you submit). Anthropic Privacy Policy. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through the API is not used to train its models.
  • Cloudflare (hosts the server that forwards your requests). Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics for usage events, Remote Config for app settings). Firebase Privacy and Security
  • RevenueCat (subscription and purchase state). RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple StoreKit (in-app purchases and subscriptions). Apple Privacy Policy

These providers may store data on servers outside your country of residence. We do not share information with any other third parties except where required by law.

4. Data retention

Text you submit is processed to return your result and is not kept on our server afterwards. Documents, keyboard settings and other app data stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app. Anthropic may retain API inputs for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring under its own policy. Analytics and purchase data is retained by the third-party services for the periods set out in their policies, after which it is deleted or aggregated. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request.

5. Children

Grammar Check is not directed at children under 13 years of age (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, to object to processing, and to data portability.

To request deletion of data associated with your device, or to exercise any other right, email us at info@slideai.ai. We will respond within a reasonable time and may ask for information needed to identify the relevant data.

You can stop text from being sent at any time: turn off "Allow Full Access" for the keyboard (Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Grammar Checker), turn off the Safari extension (Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions), or simply don't use the checking features.

7. Security

Information sent from the app is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to info@slideai.ai.
